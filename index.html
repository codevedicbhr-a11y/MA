Welcome to Mary's Academy

Excellence in English Medium Education

Located at Hanuman Chowk, Sarhad (Near Baba Hardware), Madhubani, Bihar, Mary's Academy provides quality English medium education in a safe, joyful and stimulating environment.

Under the leadership of Director Mary Jha and Principal Anuradha Kumari, we focus on academic excellence, character building, sports and life skills so every child can shine.
